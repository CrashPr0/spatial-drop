How to scan: put the object on a textured table in even light and tap Start. The scan walks you through three steps: around the object at its height, around again from above, then a few photos over the top. Follow the arrows and keep moving slowly: sharp views are picked as you go, and the guide tells you when to slow down. The ring below the camera fills as each side is photographed. Follow the outlined section to fill a missed angle.
